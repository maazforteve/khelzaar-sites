Towerglow

Tap in time, build high

Coming soon to Google Play

Towerglow is a one-tap timing game. A block slides above your tower — tap to drop it. Anything hanging over the edge is sliced off, so the next block is narrower. Line it up perfectly and it keeps its full width.

  • Five perfect drops in a row grow your block back.
  • The sky changes from day to sunset to night as your tower climbs.
  • Speed rises with every block — how high can you go?
  • Revive once and keep stacking.
  • Rewards every ten blocks and for each perfect chain.
  • Block themes: Sunny, Woodwork and Candy Glass, plus a Night City skyline.
