XOtwist

Tic tac toe with a twist

Coming soon to Google Play

Three in a row has never felt this fresh. XOtwist is the classic X and O game you know, rebuilt with a clean design, a smart AI and a twist that changes everything.

  • Four AI levels — Easy for a relaxed round, Medium and Hard for a real challenge, and Unbeatable for perfect play. Hold it to a draw if you can.
  • Vanish mode — each side keeps only three marks. Place a fourth and your oldest mark fades away, so there are no full boards and every move matters.
  • Two players — pass the phone and play a friend face to face.
  • Rounds alternate who starts, with a running score for X, O and draws.
  • Hints and undo when you need them, and a clear winning-line animation.
  • Daily rewards, daily missions, achievements and 20 player levels.
  • Board skins: Neon Night, Fire & Ice and the animated Aurora board.
