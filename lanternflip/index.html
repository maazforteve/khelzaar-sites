Lanternflip

Light every lantern

Coming soon to Google Play

Lanternflip is a glowing logic puzzle. Every tap flips a lantern and the four lanterns next to it — light every lantern to finish the garden.

  • A hundred gardens growing from 3×3 to 6×6, plus a fresh 5×5 Daily garden.
  • Par is the fewest taps possible: meet it for three stars.
  • A hint rings a lantern on the shortest way to light them all.
  • Daily rewards, missions and achievements.
  • Lantern styles: Amber, Jade and Rose.
