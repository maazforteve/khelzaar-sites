RideLine - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

RideLine: Physics Bike Racing ("RideLine", "the game") is published by Inflo Byte.

What we collect

Nothing. RideLine does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and makes no network requests of its own.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, bikes, settings, best-run ghosts) is saved in the game's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Clips and photos

If you choose to save a replay clip or photo, the file is created on your device and you decide whether to share it through your device's share menu. We never receive it.

Permissions

RideLine does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or personal files.

Children

The game is rated for a general audience and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
