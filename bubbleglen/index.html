Bubbleglen

Aim, bounce and pop

Coming soon to Google Play

Bubbleglen is a gentle bubble shooter in a pastel valley. Drag to aim, let go to shoot, and bounce shots off the walls to reach tricky spots.

  • Three or more of a colour pop; anything left hanging falls for double points.
  • Shots that pop nothing fill the misses meter and bring a new row down.
  • Clear the glen before the bubbles cross the dashed line.
  • Sixty levels, plus an Endless glen where the rows keep coming.
  • Rainbow and bomb boosters for stubborn clusters.
  • Daily rewards, missions and achievements.
  • Bubble and backdrop styles: Candy Gloss, Gemstones, Lagoon and Dusk.
