Crownleap

Leap, capture, get crowned

Coming soon to Google Play

Crownleap is checkers (draughts) on a clean board with a clever AI. Move diagonally, jump to capture and reach the far row to be crowned.

  • Three AI levels: Easy, Medium and Hard.
  • Two variants: Classic, and Flying kings that glide any distance.
  • Captures are compulsory and chains must be finished — glowing squares show your options.
  • Two players on one phone.
  • Hint and take-back helpers against the AI.
  • Pieces and boards: Ivory & Ebony, Gold & Silver, Walnut and Marble Hall.
