Letterloon

Guess letters, save balloons

Coming soon to Google Play

Letterloon is a gentle, colourful word game. A little flyer floats on six balloons above the sea. Guess the hidden word one letter at a time — right letters fill the blanks, wrong ones pop a balloon. Solve it before the last balloon pops!

  • Eight categories: Animals, Countries, Pakistan, Food, Sports, Science, Jobs and Nature — or play them all mixed.
  • Hundreds of words, never the same word twice in a row.
  • Reveal a letter or add an extra balloon when a word is tough.
  • Word streaks: every 10 words in a row pays a bonus.
  • Daily rewards, missions and achievements.
  • Dress your flyer in a cap or a hero cape, and fly in day, night or desert skies.
