Snakegrove

Eat, grow, don't bite

Coming soon to Google Play

Snakegrove brings the classic snake game to a bright garden. Swipe to steer, eat apples to grow, and never bite your own tail.

  • Classic — solid walls, pure skill.
  • Wrap — leave one side of the field and come back on the other.
  • Maze — obstacles in the field, with a new layout every run.
  • Golden apples worth 30 points appear after every fifth apple — grab them before they vanish.
  • Speed builds as you grow. Smooth movement, no lag.
  • Shield and slow-motion boosters, plus one revive per run.
  • Swipe controls or on-screen arrows.
  • Snake skins: Coral, Tiger Stripes and an animated Galaxy, plus a sparkle trail.
