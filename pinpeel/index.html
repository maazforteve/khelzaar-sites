Pinpeel

Unscrew, swing, drop

Coming soon to Google Play

Pinpeel is a wooden screw puzzle. Lift a screw and put it in an empty hole: a plank held by one screw swings down, a plank held by none drops away.

  • A screw under another plank is stuck until that plank is gone — order matters.
  • 120 boards from Workbench to Master joiner, each checked solvable with its fewest moves as par.
  • Undo, reset and hints; a daily board for everyone.
  • Board styles: Oak, Maple and Ebony.
