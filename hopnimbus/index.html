Hopnimbus

Hop Nimbo to the stars

Coming soon to Google Play

Hopnimbus is an endless climb with a cheerful little robot. Nimbo hops by himself — you steer left and right, and wrap from one side of the screen to the other.

  • Springs launch you high; crumbly clouds give way; storm clouds zap.
  • Stomp hailstones from above — touch them any other way and you fall.
  • Grab stars, propellers and shields.
  • Daily sky: the same clouds for everyone, once a day.
  • Start with a propeller, or revive on a fresh cloud after a fall.
  • Best heights, daily missions and achievements.
  • Nimbo and cloud styles: Mint Nimbo, Sunny Nimbo and Cotton Candy.
