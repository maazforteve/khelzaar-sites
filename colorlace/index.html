Colorlace

Lace pairs, fill the board

Coming soon to Google Play

Colorlace is a soothing logic puzzle. Drag from a dot to lace it to the other dot of the same colour — laces never cross, and every square must be filled.

  • 150 levels with grids that grow as you go, plus a fresh 7×7 Daily lace.
  • Drawing over a lace cuts it, so you can rethink any path.
  • Use no more moves than colours for three stars.
  • A hint lays one lace exactly where it belongs.
  • Daily rewards, missions and achievements.
  • Lace and board styles: Pastel Laces, Neon Laces, Paper Board and Midnight Board.
