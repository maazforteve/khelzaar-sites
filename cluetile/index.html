Cluetile is a word-deduction puzzle with tiles that talk back. Guess a real word and every tile tells you something: a dark tile is in the right spot, a coral tile is in the word somewhere else, and a grey tile is not in the word at all.

  • Daily word — one five-letter word for everyone, every day. Build a streak and compare your guess spread.
  • Ladder — climb level by level as words grow from four to six letters.
  • Rush — solve as many four-letter words as you can in three minutes; each solve adds time.
  • Hard mode for experts: found letters must be used in every later guess.
  • Reveal-a-letter and extra-guess helpers when you are stuck.
  • Colour-blind mode adds a dot and a ring to every clue.
  • Share your daily result, earn coins, unlock tile and keyboard styles.
