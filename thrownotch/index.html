Thrownotch

Time it, throw it

Coming soon to Google Play

Thrownotch is a one-tap timing game.

  • Tap to throw a knife into the spinning target. Hit a knife that is already stuck and the run is over.
  • Throw every knife to break the target; the next one spins faster, stops and starts, or swings back.
  • Every fifth target is a boss: Cheese Wheel, Brass Gong, Iron Shield and War Drum.
  • Slice apples on the rim for coins; one second chance per run.
  • A daily run of five targets, the same for everyone.
  • Knife styles: Steel, Brass and Obsidian.
