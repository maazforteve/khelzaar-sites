Crosspetal

Tiny crosswords, daily mini

Coming soon to Google Play

Crosspetal is the crossword for a coffee break. Buds are 3×3, petals are 5×5, and every answer crosses others.

  • A hundred puzzles plus a new daily mini.
  • Type with the keys below; the clue sits right above them.
  • Tap the same square again to switch between Across and Down.
  • Solve without reveals for three stars.
  • Letter, word and check helpers when you are stuck.
  • Grid styles: Rose Paper, Newsprint and Midnight.
