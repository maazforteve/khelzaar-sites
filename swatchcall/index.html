Swatchcall

Match colours, call SWATCH

Coming soon to Google Play

Swatchcall is a fast colour-matching card game. Match the top card by colour, number or symbol — wilds fit anything — and be the first to empty your hand.

  • Skip, Reverse, +2 and Wild +4 bend the turn order.
  • Tap SWATCH! when you are down to two cards, or get caught and draw two.
  • Play one, two or three AI rivals on Easy, Medium or Hard.
  • A wild-card booster for a tight spot.
  • Daily rewards, missions and achievements.
  • Deck styles: Sorbet, Paper and Midnight.
