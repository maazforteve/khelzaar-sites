Privacy Policy for Solicove
Last updated: 2 November 2026

Solicove is a game published by Digi Info Tech ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

1. Data stored on your device
Your progress, coins, skins, settings and statistics are saved only on your device. We do not run accounts or servers for Solicove and we do not receive this data. Uninstalling the app or using Settings > Reset progress deletes it.

2. Advertising
The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Purchases
Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive a confirmation of the item bought and a purchase ID, never your card or payment details.

4. Children
Solicove is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Security and retention
Data on your device stays there until you delete it. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own policies.

6. Changes
We may update this policy. The latest version is always available in the app and on this website.
