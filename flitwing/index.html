Flitwing

One tap, one flap

Coming soon to Google Play

Flitwing is one-tap flying at its simplest. Tap to flap, let go to fall, and thread your bird through the gaps in the cloud columns.

  • Every gate is a point; golden feathers in some gaps are worth two more.
  • Medals at 10, 25, 50 and 100 gates.
  • The sky turns from summer blue to sunset as you climb.
  • A shield booster absorbs one crash.
  • Coins every ten gates, daily missions and achievements.
  • Birds and skies to unlock: Blue Jay, Night Owl, Phoenix, Sunset Glow and Starry Night.
