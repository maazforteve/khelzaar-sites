Combspell

Spell words from seven letters

Coming soon to Google Play

Combspell is a word puzzle in a honeycomb. Seven letters, one golden centre: spell as many words of four or more letters as you can — every word must use the centre letter.

  • Letters may repeat; longer words score more.
  • Use all seven letters for a pangram: +7.
  • Fifty-one hives. Stars unlock new ones, and reaching Queen masters a hive.
  • A fresh Daily hive every day.
  • A hint shows the start and length of a word you are missing.
  • Daily rewards, missions and achievements.
  • Hive styles: Honeycomb, Mint Garden and Ember Night.
