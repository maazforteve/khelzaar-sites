Cometvolley

Shatter comets, beat the core

Coming soon to Google Play

Cometvolley is a space shooter you play with one thumb. Drag to fly — your guns fire by themselves — and shatter the comets before they reach you.

  • Big comets split when hit; drones shoot back.
  • Every fifth wave a core boss arrives.
  • Grab spread, rapid, shield and nova drops.
  • Three hits and the run is over — or revive once with a shield.
  • Best waves and scores, daily missions and achievements.
  • Ships and shots: Ember Dart, Void Runner, Mint Bolts and Nova Pink.
