Blocksnug

Slot blocks, clear lines

Coming soon to Google Play

Blocksnug is a calm wood-block puzzle. Drag pieces from the tray onto the 9×9 board and fill a row, a column or a 3×3 box to clear it.

  • Clear several at once for a combo; clear on back-to-back moves for a streak.
  • Classic has no clock — the game ends when no piece in the tray fits.
  • Blitz gives you two minutes; the Daily board is the same for everyone.
  • Refresh swaps your three pieces; Bomb clears the fullest box.
  • Best scores, daily missions and achievements.
  • Block and board styles: Jewel Tones, Neon Pop, Birch Board and Slate Board.
