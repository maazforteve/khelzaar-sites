Pairglade

Flip, remember, match

Coming soon to Google Play

Pairglade is a fresh take on the classic memory card game. Turn two cards over — a matching pair stays up, anything else flips back. Remember where everything is and clear the board.

  • Levels — boards grow from 3×4 up to 6×6 with a timer and a move target for bonus coins.
  • Quick play — pick any board size and relax with no timer.
  • Two players — take turns on one phone; a match earns a point and another turn.
  • Peek and extra-time helpers.
  • Smooth 3D card flips and clear, colourful card faces.
  • Daily missions, achievements and player levels.
  • Card sets: Garden, Midnight, Candy and Golden.
