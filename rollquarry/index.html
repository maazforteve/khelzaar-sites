Rollquarry

Five dice, thirteen boxes

Coming soon to Google Play

Rollquarry is the classic five-dice scorecard game with a quarry twist. Roll up to three times a turn, hold the dice you like and write the result into one empty box.

  • Ones to Sixes: reach 63 for a +35 bonus.
  • Sets, runs and the Motherlode — five alike is worth 50.
  • Solo quarry for your best haul, a Daily quarry with the same dice for everyone, or Duel the AI.
  • Two players on one phone.
  • An extra-roll booster for a fourth throw.
  • Dice and trays: Ember Dice, Obsidian & Gold, Saddle Leather and Geode.
