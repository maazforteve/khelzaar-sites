Bounceflare

Thirty levels of bricks

Coming soon to Google Play

Bounceflare is a bright, punchy brick breaker. Slide the paddle, serve the ball and smash every brick on the board.

  • Thirty levels, from first serves to steel mazes.
  • Tough bricks take several hits; steel never breaks — find the way around it.
  • Capsules drop a wider paddle, extra balls, a slower ball or an extra life.
  • Wide-paddle, multi-ball and extra-ball boosters for the hard levels.
  • Coins for every clear, more for later levels and a bonus for not losing a ball.
  • Ball and brick styles: Ice Shard, Gold Rush, Candy Bricks and Pixel Arcade.
