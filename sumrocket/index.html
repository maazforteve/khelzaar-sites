Sumrocket turns mental maths into a race. Pick the right answer before the bar runs out — every correct answer adds time and builds your streak multiplier, up to ×5.

  • Add & subtract — from single digits up to the hundreds.
  • Mixed — plus, minus, times and divide, always with exact answers.
  • Times tables — practise any table from 2 to 12, or a random mix.
  • Starter — single digits and a slower bar for beginners.
  • Three hearts: a wrong answer costs a heart and three seconds.
  • Extra time and skip helpers, plus a second chance.
  • Themes: Lagoon, Chalkboard and Neon Grid, plus avatars and frames.
