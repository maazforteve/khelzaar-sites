Wordswivel

Spin letters, solve clues

Coming soon to Google Play

Wordswivel is an anagram puzzle with a twist: every word comes with a clue. Tap the letters on the ring to spell the answer, and swivel the ring when you need to see them in a new order.

  • Solve three words to clear a level; words grow from four to eight letters.
  • Three hearts — a wrong guess or a timeout costs one.
  • Reveal locks the next letter into place; Skip moves on without losing a heart.
  • Coins every level and a big bonus every tenth.
  • Daily rewards, missions and achievements.
  • Tile and room styles: Wooden Tiles, Marble Tiles and Night Lounge.
