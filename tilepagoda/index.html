Tilepagoda

Match tiles, clear the stack

Coming soon to Google Play

Tilepagoda is classic mahjong solitaire, calm and clear on a phone screen.

  • Tap two matching tiles to take them off. A tile is free when nothing lies on it and its left or right side is open.
  • Dots, bamboo, numbers, winds, dragons, flowers and seasons; any flower matches any flower, any season any season.
  • 120 boards in four packs, from Garden to Summit, ten stacked layouts from Courtyard to Turtle. Every deal can be cleared.
  • Undo, hints and shuffles when you are stuck; clear a board with no help for three stars.
  • A daily board, the same for everyone.
  • Tile sets: Jade, Lacquer and Bamboo.
