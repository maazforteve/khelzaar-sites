Dashmeadow

Jump, slide, never stop

Coming soon to Google Play

Dashmeadow is an endless runner through a sunset meadow. Swipe left and right to change lanes, up to jump fences and down to slide under arches.

  • Grab seeds for points and power-ups: magnet, shield and double score.
  • The meadow speeds up the further you run.
  • Daily dash: the same course for everyone, once a day.
  • Start with a shield, or revive once after a crash.
  • Best distance, daily missions and achievements.
  • Runners and worlds: Silver Hare, Panda Sprinter, Dawn Meadow and Moonlit Meadow.
