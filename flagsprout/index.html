Flagsprout

Minesweeper with no guessing

Coming soon to Google Play

Flagsprout is minesweeper the way it should be: fair. Every meadow can be cleared with logic alone — no coin-flip guesses at the end — and your first dig is always safe.

  • Tap to dig. A number tells you how many thistle mines touch that tile.
  • Long-press, or switch to Flag mode, to plant a flag on a mine.
  • Easy, Medium and Hard meadows, each checked by a solver before you play.
  • Safe-dig and extra-life helpers for tricky spots.
  • Best times, daily rewards, missions and achievements.
  • Desert Dunes grid and Blossom Flags to unlock.
