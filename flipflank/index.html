Flipflank

Trap the line, own the board

Coming soon to Google Play

Flipflank is reversi in black and gold. Place a disc so it traps a line of rival discs against one of yours — every trapped disc flips to your colour.

  • Lines run along rows, columns and diagonals.
  • Three AI levels: Easy, Medium and Hard.
  • Quick 6×6 and Classic 8×8 boards.
  • Two players on one phone.
  • Hint and take-back helpers when you play the AI.
  • Disc and board styles: Black & White, Ember & Teal, Club Green and Ivory Salon.
