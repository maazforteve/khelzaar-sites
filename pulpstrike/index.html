Pulpstrike

Slice fruit, dodge bombs

Coming soon to Google Play

Pulpstrike is fast, juicy fruit slicing.

  • Swipe to cut watermelons, oranges, apples, limes, coconuts, plums and peaches in two.
  • Three or more in one swipe is a combo bonus.
  • Classic: three dropped fruit or one bomb ends the round.
  • Arcade: 60 seconds, golden star fruit start a frenzy. Zen: 90 calm seconds, no bombs.
  • A daily round with the same throws for everyone.
  • Blade styles: Steel, Ember and Violet.
