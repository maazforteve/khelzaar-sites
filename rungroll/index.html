Rungroll

Ladders, snakes, pick a die

Coming soon to Google Play

Rungroll is the family classic made bright and quick. Roll, hop square by square, ride the ladders up and slide down the snakes on your way to 100.

  • Classic mode — roll and go, just like the board game.
  • Pick-a-die mode — roll two dice and choose one, so every turn is a small decision.
  • Play against one to three bots, or pass the phone around for up to four friends.
  • A six rolls again. Land exactly on 100 to win — too high and you bounce back.
  • Token hops, ladder climbs and snake slides you can follow at a glance.
  • Daily rewards, missions and achievements.
  • Tokens, boards and snakes to unlock: Gem Drops, Star Tokens, Night Garden and Rainbow Snakes.
