Tilefuse

Slide, merge and climb

Coming soon to Google Play

Swipe, slide and fuse equal tiles into bigger numbers. Tilefuse is a merge puzzle with two ways to play.

  • Classic — pick a board from 3×3 to 6×6 and chase milestone tiles. Your board is saved, so you can always resume.
  • Quests — 30 handmade challenges with stone blocks that never move, target tiles and move limits. Earn up to three stars on each.
  • Undo a swipe or remove a tile when you are cornered.
  • Smooth slide and merge animations, with keyboard arrows on bigger screens.
  • Daily missions, achievements and player levels.
  • Tile sets: Neon Gems, Candy and Ocean, plus an animated Starfield board.
